Medien1 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

Medien1 is an AI-powered media planning and advertising automation platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when users connect advertising accounts or use our campaign planning tools.

Information We Collect

We may collect basic account information such as name, email address, company details, advertising account IDs, campaign information, audience inputs, campaign objectives, budgets, creative text, and reporting data. If a user connects Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, or Pinterest Ads, we may process authorized advertising account data through secure API access.

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to generate media plans, create campaign structures, prepare bulk upload files, validate campaign readiness, create paused advertising campaigns, retrieve campaign performance reports, and improve the user experience. We do not sell user data.

Advertising Platforms Data Usage (Google, Meta, LinkedIn, Pinterest)

If a user authorizes access to Google Ads, Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, or Pinterest Ads, Medien1 may use these platforms' data only for campaign planning, campaign setup, account validation, reporting, and optimization features requested by the user.

We access only the permissions approved by the user during the OAuth authorization process for each respective network.

  • Google Ads: Medien1’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Meta Ads, LinkedIn Ads, & Pinterest Ads: Data obtained via these APIs is used strictly to provide the Medien1 service and is not sold, transferred, or used for unauthorized profiling, in accordance with their respective developer terms.

Data Sharing

We do not share personal or advertising account data with unauthorized third parties. Data may be shared only when required to provide the requested service, comply with legal requirements, or operate integrations with authorized advertising platforms.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. API tokens and connected account credentials are encrypted, handled securely, and used only for authorized platform actions.

User Control & Access Revocation

Users can revoke Medien1's platform access at any time directly from the respective advertising platform's security settings:

  • Google: Revoke access via your Google Account Security settings.
  • Meta: Revoke access via your Facebook/Business Integrations settings.
  • LinkedIn: Revoke access via your LinkedIn Account's Permitted Services settings.
  • Pinterest: Revoke access via your Pinterest Account's Connected Apps settings.

Users may also contact us directly to request the immediate deletion or removal of all stored account information and disconnected API data.
