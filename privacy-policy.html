Medien1 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 June 2026

Medien1 is an AI-powered media planning and advertising automation platform. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when users connect advertising accounts or use our campaign planning tools.

Information We Collect

We may collect basic account information such as name, email address, company details, advertising account IDs, campaign information, audience inputs, campaign objectives, budgets, creative text, and reporting data. If a user connects LinkedIn Ads, Google Ads, Meta Ads, or other advertising platforms, we may process authorized advertising account data through secure API access.

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to generate media plans, create campaign structures, prepare bulk upload files, validate campaign readiness, create paused advertising campaigns, retrieve campaign performance reports, and improve the user experience. We do not sell user data.

LinkedIn Ads Data Usage

If a user authorizes LinkedIn access, Medien1 may use LinkedIn Ads data only for campaign planning, campaign setup, account validation, reporting, and optimization features requested by the user. We access only the permissions approved by the user during the LinkedIn OAuth authorization process.

Data Sharing

We do not share personal or advertising account data with unauthorized third parties. Data may be shared only when required to provide the requested service, comply with legal requirements, or operate integrations with authorized advertising platforms.

Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure. API tokens and connected account credentials should be handled securely and used only for authorized platform actions.

User Control

Users can revoke platform access at any time from the respective advertising platform settings. Users may also contact us to request deletion or removal of stored information.
